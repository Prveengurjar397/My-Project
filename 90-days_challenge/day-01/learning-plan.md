## My Understanding of DevOps

DevOps is a mindset, culture and set of practices that helps development and operations teams work together to build, test, deploy and maintain applications efficiently and reliably.

## My Understanding of Cloud

Cloud computing provides computing resources such as servers, storage and networking over the internet, allowing applications to be deployed and managed without owning physical infrastructure.

## Why I Am Learning DevOps & Cloud

I am learning DevOps and Cloud to upgrade my skills and move from being a fresher to a highly skilled IT professional.

My ultimate goal is to build strong practical skills, gain real-world experience through projects and secure a good DevOps job.

## Where I Want to Reach

I want to become a successful DevOps and Cloud professional with strong Linux and System Administration skills.

I want to become strong in automation and master important DevOps tools through practical projects.

## My 3 Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. Become strong in Linux and System Administration.
2. Learn important DevOps tools and focus on automation.
3. Become job-ready for a DevOps Engineer role through practical projects and interview preparation.
